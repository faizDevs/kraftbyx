Idea se delivery tak — 4 steps, zero confusion.

  1. 01Ignite

    We listen. Aapka idea, goals, audience — sab samajhte hain. Ek cup chai, poori clarity.

    DURATION: 1–2 DAYS

  2. 02Heat

    Research + concepts + prototypes. Design direction lock hoti hai yahin.

    DURATION: 3–7 DAYS

  3. 03Forge

    Actual build — design, code, development. Daily progress updates.

    DURATION: PROJECT-BASED

  4. 04Ship

    Testing, polish, delivery + post-launch support. Project close nahi hota, complete hota hai.

    DURATION: 1–3 DAYS

> KNOWLEDGE BASE: FAQ_

Questions, Answered

Kraftbyx is a digital forge — ek studio jo logos aur brand graphics design karta hai, PC aur mobile games banata hai, websites aur apps ship karta hai, aur interactive PC wallpapers craft karta hai. India se remote-first kaam karte hain, aur clients globally serve karte hain.

Paanch modules: Design Forge, Game Forge, Web Forge, App Forge aur Live Walls. Har module standalone bhi available hai — aap sirf design le sakte ho, sirf website, ya mix kar sakte ho. Jaisa project demand kare.

Brief finalize hone ke 48 hours ke andar kickstart hota hai. Pehle 1–2 din Ignite phase mein goals, audience aur scope lock karte hain. Uske baad Heat phase mein research aur concepts aate hain, jahan design direction final hoti hai.

Haan — har package mein defined revision rounds included hain. Revision rounds package size pe depend karte hain. Brief sign-off ke baad scope change ho toh hum upfront bata dete hain, surprise nahi.

50% advance, 50% delivery pe. Bade projects ke liye milestone payments — har phase complete hone pe bill hota hai. Final files aur source handover project payment ke baad hota hai, sab invoice ke saath.

Bilkul. Har module standalone available hai — mix bhi kar sakte ho. Kai clients sirf logo ya brand graphics lete hain, ya sirf website, aur development apni team ke paas rakhte hain.

India se, remote-first. Clients globally serve karte hain, MON–SAT 10:00–19:00 IST. Communication email aur WhatsApp pe hoti hai, aur build phase mein daily progress updates milte hain.
